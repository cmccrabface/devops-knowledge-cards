# Marketing Launch Strategy — DevOps Knowledge Cards

## 🎯 Target Audience
- Junior to Senior DevOps/SRE engineers preparing for interviews
- Career switchers moving into cloud/DevOps roles
- Bootcamp graduates looking to validate knowledge
- Engineers studying for AWS/CKA/Terraform certifications

## 📅 Launch Timeline

### Week 1: Pre-Launch (Build Buzz)
- [ ] Post "building in public" thread on Twitter/X
- [ ] Share on r/devops, r/sysadmin, r/aws, r/kubernetes (value-first, not spammy)
- [ ] Write a Dev.to article: "100 DevOps Interview Questions That Actually Matter"
- [ ] Create a LinkedIn post about the problem (interview prep is broken)
- [ ] Record a 60-second demo video/GIF

### Week 2: Launch Day
- [ ] Post on Hacker News (Show HN: DevOps flashcards with spaced repetition)
- [ ] Product Hunt launch
- [ ] Tweet thread: "I built 100+ DevOps flashcards, here are the 10 hardest questions 🧵"
- [ ] Cross-post to DevOps Slack/Discord communities
- [ ] Email to personal network

### Week 3-4: Sustain & Iterate
- [ ] Respond to all comments and feedback
- [ ] Add questions requested by community
- [ ] Write follow-up articles (e.g., "How I'd Prep for a Senior SRE Interview")
- [ ] Reach out to DevOps newsletters for features
- [ ] A/B test pricing page copy

## 📣 Content Strategy

### Blog Posts (Dev.to / Hashnode / Medium)
1. "100 DevOps Interview Questions That Actually Matter" (lead magnet)
2. "Why Spaced Repetition is the Best Way to Study for Technical Interviews"
3. "The DevOps Knowledge Map: What You Need to Know at Each Level"
4. "From Junior to Senior DevOps: A Study Roadmap"

### Social Media
- **Twitter/X:** Daily DevOps tips (1 question + answer per day) → link to app
- **LinkedIn:** Weekly career-focused posts about DevOps interview prep
- **Reddit:** Genuine participation in DevOps communities, share as a resource when relevant

### SEO
- Target keywords: "devops interview questions", "sre interview prep", "kubernetes interview", "aws interview questions"
- Landing page optimized for these terms
- Blog posts targeting long-tail keywords

## 💰 Monetization Strategy

### Free Tier (Acquisition)
- 20 cards (sampling across all categories)
- Full flip animations and progress tracking
- Goal: demonstrate value, hook users

### Pro Tier — $9 one-time (Revenue)
- All 100+ cards
- Spaced repetition algorithm
- Priority content updates
- Goal: convert engaged free users

### Future Revenue Streams
- **Premium categories:** CI/CD, Linux, Cloud Architecture ($4.99 each)
- **Team licenses:** $49/team for hiring managers to share with candidates
- **Enterprise:** White-label for bootcamps and training companies
- **Certification packs:** AWS SAA, CKA, Terraform Associate specific prep

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track
- Landing page → app conversion rate
- Free → Pro conversion rate
- Daily active users
- Cards studied per session
- Retention (D1, D7, D30)
- Organic traffic growth

## 🏆 Competitive Advantage
1. **Focused:** DevOps/SRE only (not generic CS)
2. **Curated:** Expert-written, not AI-generated spam
3. **Smart:** Spaced repetition adapts to your knowledge gaps
4. **Beautiful:** Clean design that people actually want to use
5. **Affordable:** $9 one-time vs $20+/month subscriptions
